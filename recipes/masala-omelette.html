Masala Omelette

Description

Masala omelette is a spiced Indian-style egg omelette.

It is made with chopped onions, tomatoes, green chillies, fresh coriander, and warm spices like turmeric, red chilli powder, and cumin. It can be made using everyday pantry spices or a ready-made omelette masala blend.

Ingredients(2 Servings)

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 small tomato, finely chopped
  • 1 green chili, finely chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon oil or butter

Steps

  1. Mix: Beat 4 eggs with 1/2 teaspoon salt, then stir in 1 small onion, finely chopped, 1 small tomato, finely chopped and 1 green chili, finely chopped.
  2. Cook: Heat 1 tablespoon oil or butter in a pan, pour in the mixture and cook on medium heat until the bottom is set.
  3. Flip and serve: Flip, cook briefly on the other side, and serve hot with toast.
