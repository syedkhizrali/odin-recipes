Lasagna

Description

Lasagna (also spelled lasagne) is a classic Italian pasta dish

It is made by stacking wide, flat sheets of pasta alternating with rich fillings like meat ragù, vegetables, béchamel or ricotta cheese, and tomato sauce, then baking it in the oven

Ingredients

  • 6 lasagna sheets
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 250 grams ground beef (or crumbled paneer/mushrooms for vegetarian)
  • 400 milliliters tomato passata
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 200 grams ricotta (or cottage cheese)
  • 150 grams grated mozzarella

Steps

  1. Preheat oven: Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Cook the base: Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a pan, then cook 1 small onion, finely chopped and 2 garlic cloves, minced until soft. Add 250 grams ground beef (or crumbled paneer/mushrooms for vegetarian) and cook until browned.
  3. Make the sauce: Stir in 400 milliliters tomato passata, 1 teaspoon dried oregano and 1 teaspoon salt. Simmer until slightly thickened.
  4. Layer: Spread a thin layer of sauce in a small baking dish. Add 2 lasagna sheets, a third of the sauce, a third of 200 grams ricotta (or cottage cheese) and a third of 150 grams grated mozzarella. Repeat twice more, finishing with cheese on top.
  5. Bake: Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake until the top is golden.
  6. Rest: Let it rest before cutting so the layers hold together.
